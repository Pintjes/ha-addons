The app does not answer

Home Assistant cannot reach the app. Check that the computer or server is on, that the app is running, and that the firewall lets Home Assistant through.

L'application ne répond pas

Home Assistant ne parvient pas à joindre l'application. Vérifiez que l'ordinateur ou le serveur est allumé, que l'application est lancée et que le pare-feu laisse passer Home Assistant.
